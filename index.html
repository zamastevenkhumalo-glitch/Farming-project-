About Us

New Age Farming Projects (Pty) Ltd began operating in 2021 and formally registered in 2024. We operate in Carolina, Mpumalanga, and are managed by Zakhele Mfanukhona Sibande.

We are a small vegetable farming project currently utilizing 0.5 hectares, with an additional 6 hectares recently acquired due to the growing demand for our vegetable produce. Our company manages vegetable crop fields with the commitment, skill, and resources to meet the requirements of our customers.

Our aim is to deliver customer satisfaction through high quality and top-class service at competitive and affordable prices. Our team is experienced, well-trained, and professional, always friendly and courteous. By dedicating ourselves to knowing our customers and their needs, we provide services that build sustainable working relationships based on mutual trust, understanding, honesty, and confidence.

We also aim to train the youth who have no opportunities to continue secondary education in business and management so that they may successfully support their needs.

🌱 Community Impact

We believe in giving back to our community. We proudly support local schools through our feeding scheme donations, ensuring that no child has to learn on an empty stomach.
